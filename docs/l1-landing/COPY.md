## What you get

**One calendar for every booking**
See bookings from your own website, Google Calendar, Airbnb, VRBO and Booking.com in one place. Add phone and walk-in bookings by hand.

**Email alerts**
Get an email when a booking comes in and when one is cancelled. Each evening, get a list of the guests who arrive tomorrow.

**Partners and agencies**
Track the bookings that tour operators, agencies and other partners send you, and the commission you owe them. Each partner gets their own page with their bookings and what they are owed, in cedis or dollars.

**Guest chats in one inbox** (badge: Coming soon)
Read and answer your guests' WhatsApp messages in one inbox. This is not ready yet.

## How it works

1. **Sign up.** Start your 30-day free trial and add your rooms.
2. **Connect your calendars.** Link your Airbnb, Booking.com, VRBO and Google calendars to Abeiku.
3. **See every booking.** Your bookings show up in one calendar. New ones keep coming in on their own.

## Questions

**How does the free trial work?**
You get 30 days free. We ask for a card when you sign up. We only charge it after the first 30 days.

**How do I pay?**
Pay in cedis with Paystack, or in US dollars by card.

**Can I cancel?**
Yes, cancel at any time. Cancel during the trial and you pay nothing.

**Do I have to stop using Airbnb or Booking.com?**
No. Keep using them as you do now. Abeiku shows their bookings next to your own.
